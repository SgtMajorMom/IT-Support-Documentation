# KB: Resetting MFA (Duo / PingID / Authenticator)

## Purpose
Provide end users with clear steps to reset their Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) method when they get a new device, lose access to their old device, or encounter MFA errors.

## When to Use This KB
Use this guide when:
- You replaced your phone and MFA no longer works
- You deleted the MFA app by accident
- You are not receiving MFA prompts
- You lost your device and need MFA reset
- You see “Device not recognized” or “Approval denied” errors

---

## 1. Contact IT Support
To protect your account, MFA resets must be performed by IT Support.

Provide:
- Your full name
- Your username or email address
- Whether you still have access to your old device
- Whether the device was lost or stolen

---

## 2. IT Support Performs the Reset
IT Support will:
- Verify your identity
- Remove your existing MFA device from the system
- Reset your MFA enrollment
- Confirm when your account is ready for re‑enrollment

---

## 3. Re-Enroll Your MFA Device
Follow the steps below based on your MFA provider.

### Duo Mobile
1. Install **Duo Mobile** from the App Store or Google Play  
2. Log in to your account  
3. When prompted, scan the QR code  
4. Approve the test push notification

### PingID
1. Install **PingID** from the App Store or Google Play  
2. Log in to your account  
3. When prompted, scan the QR code  
4. Complete the pairing process

### Microsoft Authenticator / Google Authenticator
1. Install the authenticator app  
2. Log in to your account  
3. Scan the QR code  
4. Enter the verification code to complete setup

---

## 4. Test MFA
After setup:
- Approve a test push notification  
- Confirm you can log in without errors  
- Notify IT Support if issues continue  

---

## Expected Outcome
Your MFA device is reset and functioning correctly, allowing secure access to your account.

## Need Help?
If you still cannot authenticate, contact IT Support for additional troubleshooting.
